Consent Form

By signing below, I freely and explicitly consent to the following:

Data Collection and Use

  • I consent to the collection and processing of personal information about the patient, including identifying details and health data (medical history and genetic information), for the purposes described in the Rett Syndrome Europe Privacy Policy.
  • I understand that this data will be used to create a Rett Syndrome patient registry, to analyze patient demographics/needs, to support research and collaborations for new treatments, and to generate anonymized statistics, as outlined in the Privacy Policy.
  • I acknowledge that this includes processing of sensitive health data under GDPR, and I give my explicit consent to this processing in accordance with Article 9(2)(a) GDPR.

Consent on Behalf of Patient

  • I confirm that I am the parent or legal guardian of the patient (or otherwise have legal authority to act on the patient's behalf), and thus I am authorized to provide consent for the processing of the patient's personal and health information.
  • The patient is unable to consent for themselves due to age or incapacity, so I am acting in their best interests.
  • I understand that Rett Syndrome Europe may request verification of my legal authority to consent for the patient if necessary.

Clinical Trial Contact

  • I consent to being contacted about potential clinical trials for which the patient may be eligible, and to have relevant information (e.g. contact details and pertinent medical data) shared with trusted pharmaceutical partners or researchers conducting such trials for that purpose.
  • By accepting this form I also consent to being contacted about any clinical trials for which the patient may be eligible. I understand I can withdraw this trial-contact consent at any time by notifying Rett Syndrome Europe, and the patient’s data will remain in the registry regardless.
  • If I do consent now, I know that the patient's identifiable data will only be shared with trial organizers with my specific permission for each trial opportunity that arises.
  • I can also withdraw this particular consent at any time, independently of the general consent, by notifying Rett Syndrome Europe.

Data Privacy and Rights

  • I confirm that I have read and understood the Privacy Policy, including details on data storage, security, and my rights as a data subject or representative.
  • I am aware that the data will be stored securely and possibly transferred internationally with appropriate safeguards if needed for research collaborations.
  • I know that all anonymized data (with personal identifiers removed) may be used in aggregate for research and shared with partners, and that identifiable data (like names or contact info) will not be disclosed to any third party without my permission as noted above.
  • I have been informed of my rights under GDPR, such as the right to access the data, correct any errors, request deletion, or withdraw consent.
  • I understand I can exercise these rights at any time by contacting the registry coordinators, and that withdrawal of consent will result in the removal of the patient's data from the registry (with no penalty or loss of benefits).

By signing/agreeing, I signify that I freely consent to the processing of the patient's data as described above, and that I have the authority to provide this consent.
