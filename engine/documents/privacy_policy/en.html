Privacy Policy

Introduction

Rett Syndrome Europe, A.s.b.l. (referred to as "we," "us," or "our"), with its registered office at Avenue Guillaume 6, R.C.S. Luxembourg F1819, is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information related to Rett Syndrome patients, care givers, professionals involved in patient care, including any medical or genetic data you may provide.

Data Collection

We collect the following types of data:

  • Personal Information: Your name, contact details (email address, phone number), age, and language preference.
  • Medical and Genetic Information: Details concerning Rett Syndrome diagnosis, including medical history, genetic mutation types (MECP2), treatment history, and other relevant health information.
  • Clinical Trial Interest: Your willingness to be contacted about potential clinical trials.

Purpose of Data Collection

The data collected will be used to build a comprehensive Patient Registry for Rett Syndrome to:

  • Understand Patient Demographics and Needs: Improve our support programs and resources for patients and families.
  • Facilitate Collaborations with Pharmaceutical Companies: Advance treatment options and therapies for Rett Syndrome.
  • Identify and Contact Interested Participants for Clinical Trials: Connect you with potential clinical trial opportunities if you have provided consent.
  • Generate Anonymized Statistics for Internal Analysis: Create aggregated data reports to better understand the Rett Syndrome community.

Consent

Participation is voluntary. By providing your data, you consent to its use for the purposes described in this policy.

We offer you the option to consent to be contacted about potential clinical trials facilitated by Rett Syndrome Europe in collaboration with pharmaceutical companies. This is entirely optional and requires your explicit agreement. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at registry@rettx.eu.

Data Sharing

Anonymized Data

We may share anonymized data with pharmaceutical companies and research institutions to facilitate clinical trials and advance treatment options for Rett Syndrome.

All shared data is aggregated and does not include information that can identify you personally.

Identifiable Data

If you have explicitly consented, we may share your contact information and relevant medical data with trusted pharmaceutical partners conducting clinical trials.

This will only occur with your explicit permission for each specific instance.

Data Storage and Security

Data Security: We implement strict technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

Data Storage: Your data is securely stored in compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

Data Retention

We retain your personal and medical data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

  • General Data: Retained for the duration of your participation plus five (5) years.
  • Clinical Trial Data: Retained for the duration of the clinical trial process or until you withdraw your consent.

Upon withdrawal of consent or request for deletion, we will securely erase your data from our systems.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data and obtain a copy.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict Processing under certain conditions.
  • Object to processing, including data sharing with pharmaceutical companies.
  • Withdraw Consent for being contacted about clinical trials at any time.
  • Data Portability, receiving your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at registry@rettx.eu. We will respond to your request within one (1) month.

Communication Preferences

You may choose to receive:

  • Clinical Trial Opportunities: Information about clinical trials for which you may be eligible.
  • Organizational Updates: News about our programs, events, and resources.

Please indicate your preferences when providing your data. You can change your preferences or unsubscribe at any time by contacting us.

Children's and Vulnerable Adults' Privacy

For participants under the age of 18, consent must be provided by a parent or legal guardian, including consent for being contacted about clinical trials. We take steps to verify that appropriate consent is obtained.

For participants over the age of 18 who are unable to provide consent due to incapacity, it is the responsibility of the parent, guardian, or representative to have the appropriate legal authority, such as a deputyship or power of attorney, to provide consent and share their data. We may request evidence of this authority where required by law.

International Data Transfers

In facilitating clinical trials, your data may be transferred to trusted pharmaceutical partners located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, to protect your data during these transfers.

Data Protection Officer

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO) to oversee compliance with data protection laws. You can contact our DPO at dpo@rettx.eu for any data protection concerns.

Data Anonymization

We employ data anonymization and pseudonymization techniques to protect your identity. Personal identifiers are removed or encrypted before data is used for analysis or shared with third parties, unless you have explicitly consented to share identifiable data for clinical trial purposes.

Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach, either within our organization or involving one of our trusted partners, we will:

  • Notify you promptly.
  • Inform you of the nature of the breach and the data potentially affected.
  • Provide recommendations on steps you can take to protect yourself.
  • Notify relevant data protection authorities as required by law.

Legal Basis for Processing

Our processing of your personal and sensitive data is based on your explicit consent under GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a). This includes processing for the purposes of facilitating clinical trials if you have provided consent.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any significant changes, particularly those affecting how we share your data or collaborate with partners, will be communicated to you via email at least thirty (30) days before they take effect. If you do not agree with the changes, you may withdraw your consent and request the deletion of your data.

Language Availability

This privacy policy is available in multiple languages to ensure accessibility for all participants. Please visit our website or contact us to request a copy in your preferred language.

Regulatory Compliance

We comply with all applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), when processing and sharing your data. Collaborations with pharmaceutical companies are conducted under strict ethical guidelines to protect your privacy and rights.

Collaborations with Pharmaceutical Companies

We collaborate with reputable pharmaceutical companies dedicated to advancing treatments for Rett Syndrome. These partnerships aim to provide you with opportunities to participate in clinical trials that may offer potential benefits. We carefully select partners who share our commitment to ethical practices and patient welfare.
